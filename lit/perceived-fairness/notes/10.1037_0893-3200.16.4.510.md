# Grote 2002: Perceiving the division of family work to be unfair

## Design
Three-wave longitudinal study of couples expecting a first child. Abstract only.

## Sample
Married couples expecting their first child (size not in the abstract).

## Key results
Only what the abstract states; see Extracts. What is at stake: C2: the abstract reports that a same-sex referent hypothesis was not supported, and that perceived inequity led to more frequent comparing, which is the reverse direction. C7: enjoyment and competence at family work, a chore-level quality, predicted fairness.
