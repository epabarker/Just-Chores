# Greenstein 1996: Gender ideology and perceptions of the fairness of the division of household labor

## Design
Cross-sectional National Survey of Families and Households analysis of married women, testing a relative-deprivation model with gender ideology as a moderator. Abstract only.

## Sample
Not stated in the abstract beyond "married women" and the survey name.

## Key results
Only what the abstract states; see Extracts. What is at stake: C1, C4 and C5. The abstract says the split relates more strongly to perceived inequity for egalitarian wives and that perceived inequity relates to marital quality; the full text holds the coefficients, the variance explained and whether the split's effect on marital quality runs through perceived inequity.

## Limitations
Abstract only: nothing here was checked against the paper's tables, so every extract is abstract-only and carries no conclusion. Wives only.
