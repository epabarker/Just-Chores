# Mikula 2009: Roots and correlates of perceived injustice in the division of family work

## Design
Cross-sectional survey test of the distributive justice framework. Abstract only.

## Sample
632 dual-earner couples with young children from 3 European countries (from the abstract).

## Key results
Only what the abstract states; see Extracts. What is at stake: C2 and C3: this is the one comprehensive test of the framework (referents, justifications, appreciation) in both partners; the abstract does not say whether the actual split was controlled or give the effect sizes.

## Limitations
Abstract only: nothing here was checked against the paper's tables, so every extract is abstract-only and carries no conclusion.
