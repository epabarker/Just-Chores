# Hawkins 1995: Exploring wives' sense of fairness about family work

## Design
Cross-sectional survey test of the distributive justice framework in wives. Abstract only.

## Sample
234 dual-earner wives randomly selected from metropolitan areas of the western United States (from the abstract).

## Key results
Only what the abstract states; see Extracts. What is at stake: C2 and C3: the first empirical test of the framework Thompson proposed; the abstract says other effects ran through the split but not which of them were referents or justifications.

## Limitations
Abstract only: nothing here was checked against the paper's tables, so every extract is abstract-only and carries no conclusion.
