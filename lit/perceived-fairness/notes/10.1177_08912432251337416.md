# Urbina 2025: Perceived fairness of couples' division of housework, multi-factorial experiment

## Design
Multi-factorial vignette experiment in a nationally representative sample. Abstract only.

## Sample
1,502 US adults rating hypothetical different-sex couples (from the abstract).

## Key results
Only what the abstract states; see Extracts. What is at stake: C3: a randomised vignette is the nearest thing to a causal test of whether stated justifications (earnings, work time) move perceived fairness; the abstract gives no effect sizes, and raters judge other couples, not their own allocation.
